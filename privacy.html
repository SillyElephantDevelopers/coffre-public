Coffre Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Overview

Coffre is a password manager for iPhone, iPad and Mac that opens KeePass databases. Coffre does not require an account, collects no personal data, contains no analytics or tracking, and makes no network connections of its own. On the Mac, its sandbox does not even allow outgoing connections.

Your Database Stays Where You Put It

Your passwords are kept in a KeePass database file that you choose: on your device, in iCloud Drive, or with any storage provider you use in Files. Coffre reads and writes that file only. If the file is in iCloud Drive or with another provider, it is stored and synced by that service under its own privacy policy; Coffre never sees anything but the encrypted file.

Before each save, Coffre keeps a copy of the database file as it was, in the app's own storage on your device, for 60 days. You can restore them from Settings ▸ Backups. They are copies of the encrypted file, so they are as protected as the database itself.

Face ID, Touch ID and Optic ID

If you choose to unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or Optic ID, Coffre keeps the database key in the keychain of your device, released only to the biometrics enrolled at that time. It never leaves your device and is not synced to your other devices. Coffre never has access to your biometric data: the system only tells it whether you were recognized.

YubiKeys

If your database is protected with a YubiKey, Coffre talks to the key over USB or NFC only to answer its challenge, on your device.

Imports

When you import an export from another password manager or authenticator app, the file is read on your device and its contents are added to your database. Nothing is sent anywhere. Coffre offers to delete the plain-text export afterwards.

Spotlight, Shortcuts and the Rest of the System

Nothing from your database is given to Spotlight, Siri or Shortcuts. The only action Coffre offers there, New Login, opens Coffre's own form and returns nothing. On the Mac, Coffre's shortcuts that work from any app are registered with the system as key combinations: Coffre learns when one of them is pressed, never what else you type.

Purchases

Coffre is free. It has no in-app purchases and no advertising.
